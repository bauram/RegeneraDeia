Transparency & Provenance

Made by local hands. Giving back to the land.

Roig is rooted in the landscape of the Serra de Tramuntana. From citrus grown in Deià to the artisan process behind every bottle, it is a product shaped by local ingredients, craftsmanship and place.

We believe that transparency is part of making a responsible product. Here, you can see where our ingredients and components come from, how the value of each bottle is distributed, and how buying directly from us contributes to the land.

The economics

Where the value of your bottle goes

34%

Just over one third goes back to the land

34% goes back to the land. After the essential costs of making and selling the bottle have been covered, all remaining margin is reinvested in the care, restoration and regeneration of Deià’s agricultural, natural and cultural landscape.

32%

Just under one third supports local artisan craft

32% supports the skilled, small-scale artisan production behind Roig. This reflects the time, knowledge and care required to transform carefully sourced ingredients into a distinctive product connected to its place of origin.

~34%

The remaining third covers essential costs

The rest covers the ingredients, packaging, taxes and other costs involved in producing each bottle and bringing it to you.

Two pillars

Two pillars behind every bottle

Local artisan craft and giving back to the land are the two pillars at the heart of Roig. Together, they represent approximately two-thirds of the value of every bottle purchased directly from us. This means that most of your purchase supports the hands that make Roig and the landscape from which it comes.

Regeneration

All margin goes back to the land

Roig is designed to create value for the place it comes from. When you buy directly from us, after the essential costs of making and selling the bottle have been covered, all remaining margin goes back to the land. Every direct purchase helps support the continued care of Deià’s agricultural landscape, natural environment and cultural heritage.

Regenera Deià is promoted by FIM Fundació Iniciatives del Mediterrani, a non-profit organization registered in Spain (G16566804), headquartered at Sant Roc, 4, 07001 Palma (Illes Balears). Operating under the applicable legal and regulatory framework for foundations and non-profit entities, Regenera Deià is developed in accordance with principles of transparency, ethical practice, and responsible community impact, supporting initiatives that strengthen territorial resilience and regenerative action.
